## Approved / fixed

- **Background:** White, not black; generous whitespace.
- **Typography:** **Editorial Serif** for expressive headings; restrained sans-serif for supporting copy.
- **Layout:** Preserve the original white template's editorial composition, section hierarchy, spacing, six-pathway grid, selected-work layout, typographic rhythm and minimalism.
- **Palette:** near-black/charcoal for headings and selective elements; neutral grey supporting text; existing Pathway footprint blue approximately `#4BB4CE` used sparingly.
- **Blue:** In the approved design, blue appears in very small motifs: thin rules, tiny arrows, underlines, discreet button edge details and selected accents. **Do not add more blue, new black tiles, extra visual devices or filled blue cards just to make the blue stand out.**
- **Imagery:** Leave accurately dimensioned media slots for genuine Pathway photography and approved client work; actual assets will be produced later.
- **Copy:** Keep the separately approved master website copy authoritative. This static visual sample is not a source of final copy, route mapping, client testimonials, results, or functional contracts.

## Explicitly rejected / do not carry forward

- Later experiments that intensified the blue accents or added black tiles and additional descriptive text.
- The two alternative grey/charcoal hybrid redesigns.
- Any design changes made by Claude solely for visual preference without user approval.
